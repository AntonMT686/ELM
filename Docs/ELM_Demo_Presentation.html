The challenge

Engineering evidence is scattered. ELM connects it.

✕  Documents reviewed by e-mail, comments lost, no proof of what was approved
✕  Requirements in spreadsheets; trace matrix rebuilt by hand before every review
✕  Code, tests and defects live in separate tools with no link back to the requirement
✕  Releases depend on people remembering the checks
✓  Configurable review workflows with preparer checklists, tied to the exact content (SHA-256), digitally signed
✓  Requirements tagged straight from documents, reviewed, approved and baselined at any level
✓  Design, code (GitLab), tests and defects linked automatically: live trace graph and matrix
✓  Release and deployment gates enforced by the system, with a tamper-evident audit trail

Documents

The preparer writes the checklist before anyone reviews

✍️ Prepare→ ☑️ Write checklist→ Submit→Check→Review→ Approve→🔏 Sign→📚 Release
Document › Review checklist
  • Number, template and revision come from the document type
  • Edit online in OnlyOffice: every save is a new iteration with its own fingerprint
  • Engineering types cannot be submitted without a checklist
  • Items can be limited to specific stages; frozen during review
  • New revision starts with a copy of the checklist

N/ARemark required: why it does not apply
  • Approve is blocked until all items are answered and none is Not OK
  • Decision bound to the SHA-256 of the exact revision
  • You can never review your own document

ADA-ELM · Reports13
Built-in control

Rules the system enforces, not people remembering

🔏
Content-bound decisions

Every review, approval and signature records the SHA-256 of the exact revision. Change the content, and approval must be given again.

⚖️
Separation of duties

No one reviews, approves, verifies, closes or releases their own work.

🎯
Scoped roles

Roles granted everywhere, per directorate, per project or per breakdown item.

🗃️
Nothing overwritten

Revisions, versions and baselines are kept; approved content changes only with a reason.

⛓️
Tamper-evident audit

Who, what, when, where, before and after, for every action and download.

📴
Offline & intranet

No cloud services. Generic demo data; your own levels and names replace it.
